# Production proposal — sharper explanation

## Promise

A motion-led, approximately 60-second promotional video that begins with the
fragmentation of generic AI/video work, reveals `explainer-video-skill`, then
proves its value through its two explainer shapes and measured local pipeline.
It earns trust with a real setup concession and closes with a concise CTA and
method credit.

## Arc

1. **Fragmented workflow** — scattered prompt, audio, editor, and timing cards.
2. **Reveal the pipeline** — the fragments snap into one source-to-video rail.
3. **Concept explainer** — numbered chips and a diagram draw with narration.
4. **Solution preview** — diagram/UI split becomes animated product telemetry.
5. **Measured local pipeline** — waveform measurement drives synchronized timing.
6. **Honest comparison** — “one-click” and “auditable/local” trade-offs shown
   side-by-side, with the developer-tooling concession visible.
7. **CTA and credit** — name, four-beat CTA, and Idan Shimon method credit.

## Visual system

- **Ground:** warm editorial paper with bold ink blocks, unlike the existing
  dark green terminal promo and the light neutral explainer template.
- **Accent system:** saturated cobalt for the pipeline, vermilion for fragmented
  pain, and a restrained mint for measured/verified states.
- **Subject motifs:** step chips, source-to-video pipeline, waveform ticks,
  diagram/UI split, and explicit measurement readouts.
- **Typography:** oversized geometric sans hierarchy with JetBrains Mono used
  only for measurements; no screenshots, stock footage, or generic gradients.
- **Motion:** sharper promotional cuts and rail-driven transitions, while
  retaining seek-safe GSAP entrances and narration-led information order.

## Delivery

- 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264/AAC MP4.
- Target runtime: about 60 seconds; final duration is derived only after locked
  narration is synthesized and measured.
- Local Kokoro narration plus a deterministic FFmpeg-synthesized atmospheric
  pulse.
- Sources, durations, decisions, and QA evidence ship with the master.

## Toolchain

| Stage | Tool |
| --- | --- |
| Narration | Kokoro-82M, `af_heart`, speed 1.33, deterministic 1.4× source gain |
| Composition | standalone HTML/CSS + vendored GSAP |
| Validation/render | HyperFrames 0.7.57 |
| Music/encode/QA | source-built FFmpeg/FFprobe with libx264 |

## Approval interpretation

The user brief explicitly authorizes autonomous end-to-end execution and fixes
the promotional arc, output location, QA gates, and method credit. Final
official publication still requires the repository owner's sign-off.
