# Production proposal

## Concept

**From question to render:** a 45–75 second concept explainer that follows
one question through the skill’s measured production system.

## Arc

1. Ask how a question becomes a video.
2. Establish that an explainer teaches rather than sells.
3. Step 01 — research and lock the narration.
4. Step 02 — synthesize per scene and measure the WAVs.
5. Step 03 — derive an HTML/SVG/GSAP composition from those measurements.
6. Validate, render, and explain what source ownership enables.
7. Recap and credit Idan Shimon.

## Visual system

- Warm neutral ground, dark ink, one teal accent.
- A persistent pipeline rail gives orientation without promo-like urgency.
- Numbered step chips, SVG flow lines, measured waveform bars, code panes,
  and QA status marks communicate process.
- Calm 0.5–0.8 second entrances; no glitch, flicker, stock footage, or CTA.
- 1920×1080 at 30 fps, narration only.

## Production gates

Script lock precedes audio. All timing surfaces derive from measured WAV
durations. Then lint, browser check, every-scene midpoint inspection, high
quality render, ffprobe, volumedetect, and final-frame inspection.
